Who Im I?

My name is Venkat Narasimha, and I’m a final-year Computer Science Engineering student at Annamacharya Institute of Technology & Sciences, Hyderabad.

I’m deeply passionate about Frontend Development and love bringing ideas to life through clean, responsive, and interactive web interfaces. I work extensively with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I enjoy crafting user experiences that are both visually appealing and functional.

Over the past few years, I’ve built several projects that reflect my creativity and technical skills — including a Blinkit clone, a Pac-Man web game, and a Tic-Tac-Toe application, all of which I designed, coded, and deployed using modern web technologies. These projects helped me strengthen my understanding of DOM manipulation, game logic, responsive design, and performance optimization.

I’m a self-motivated learner who believes in improving through consistent practice, collaboration, and exploration. I regularly work on GitHub projects, experiment with new frameworks and tools, and aim to stay up to date with the ever-evolving web ecosystem. My goal is to become a proficient full-stack developer and contribute to building innovative, user-centric digital products that make an impact.

Beyond coding, I value teamwork, problem-solving, and continuous learning — qualities that help me grow both as a developer and as an individual.
